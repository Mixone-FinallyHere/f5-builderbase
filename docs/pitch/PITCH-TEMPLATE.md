# F5 - Builderbase — Pitch Template

Target: 3 minutes, 10 slides, one idea per slide. A 16:9 PowerPoint version in the palette from [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) can be generated with `pip install python-pptx && python docs/pitch/build_pitch_deck.py` (outputs `F5-Builderbase-Pitch-Template.pptx`).

| # | Slide | Goal | Fill in | Time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Title** | Name + one-line promise | Project name, tagline, team, event | 0:05 |
| 2 | **Hook** | Make them care in 10 seconds | A surprising stat, a question, or a 1-sentence story | 0:15 |
| 3 | **Problem** | Make the pain concrete | Who hurts, how often, what it costs (time/money); one real quote | 0:20 |
| 4 | **Solution** | Say what it is, plainly | "X helps [who] do [what] by [how]" + 3 key benefits | 0:20 |
| 5 | **Demo** | Show, don't tell | The single golden path in 3 steps; screenshot or live demo link; fallback video | 0:45 |
| 6 | **How it works** | Earn technical credibility | Architecture diagram, stack, what you built tonight vs. reused, the clever bit | 0:20 |
| 7 | **Market** | Show it is worth building | Who pays, TAM/SAM/SOM (or user count), competitors and your edge | 0:15 |
| 8 | **Why now** | Create urgency | The shift (tech, regulation, behavior) that makes this possible today | 0:10 |
| 9 | **Team** | Why you | Names, roles, one credential each, who did what tonight | 0:15 |
| 10 | **Ask** | Clear next step | What you want (feedback, pilot, funding, intros), contact, QR to repo/live URL | 0:15 |

## Delivery tips

- One sentence headline per slide; max ~20 words on screen.
- Rehearse the demo twice; record a fallback video.
- Finish by pointing to the live URL and repo: https://github.com/WhiteChair/f5-builderbase
